# Project «Семейные трусы» / «Semeynye Trusy»

English · [Русский](README.ru.md)

> *\* The status of this document is a project application. More detailed elaboration, including budget calculation, is the subject of further interaction.*

**Project for the Repurposing of an NGO / Institute / Think Tank in Israel**

*Due to its legal registration, domain, and developed methodology, the International Burke Institute is the most convenient foothold for organizing and conducting research into boxer shorts.*

Location: ibi.institute

Date: 22.10.2026 (Thursday, 17:00 MSK)

---

**Proposal:** Keep the abbreviation, the methodology, and the structure of the Institute, and replace the subject of research.

**Project goal:** To build a stable pool of readers (DMs, OLs, and politically active citizens) who use the Index of the Institute as an authoritative reference when choosing boxer shorts.

**Priority regions:** Portugal, Japan, and further according to the Country Register.

**Background:**

The International Burke Institute was registered in the State of Israel as a non-profit organization in July 2025. The website was launched the same year. The Institute publishes analytical materials and an annual "Sovereignty Index" ranking states, and lists the UN, UNICEF, UNESCO, SIPRI, and the World Bank as partners.

The development of the project is encountering a significant problem. From the point of view of Western media, OLs, and prominent participants in social networks, the materials of the Institute lack a legitimate subject. In particular:
- the organizations listed on the website as partners do not confirm cooperation;
- the materials are not cited by the leading Western media;
- the "Sovereignty Index" is not used by any state, including the states it ranks;
- in August 2026, the developer of the language model used to produce the materials reported their origin and blocked the related accounts;
- technical work on masking and information security has not been completed.

At the same time, one cannot fail to note that the Institute has assets that enjoy steady stability: a registered legal entity, a domain, a recognizable abbreviation, a developed methodology, an index of 7 (seven) dimensions, tier bands, and a committee structure. The loss of these assets is considered undesirable.

The following options were considered:
1. Closing the Institute. Results in the loss of the domain, the registration, and the accumulated search-engine positions. Not recommended.
2. Moving the Institute to another research subject (climate, migration, energy). Requires external data, invited experts, and confirmation from partners. Not recommended.
3. Keeping the abbreviation, the methodology, and the structure, and replacing the subject.

The above places us before the necessity of choosing option 3. It is felt that the new subject must:
- enjoy steady popularity among the audience of any country;
- be understandable without translation;
- require no external data and no invited experts;
- be compatible with the existing dimensions of the Index (sovereignty, independence, integrity, security);
- be compatible with the abbreviation IBI.

In our opinion, the best option today is boxer shorts. The letters of the abbreviation are kept: "International" is replaced by "Ilya", "Burke" by "Boxers". "Institute" is kept without alteration.

**Ideological Foundations:**

1) The boxer short is worn in every country of the world but has not yet found institutional embodiment.

2) The shared requirements of all wearers regarding comfort, durability, and discretion.

3) The boxer short as a socio-cultural phenomenon differs:
- from the brief — by greater freedom and a rejection of compression;
- from the so-called "boxer brief" — by a rejection of compromise;
- from the absence of underwear — by the presence of a standard.

4) For each audience there is an incentive to participate in the project:
- for DMs — comfort during long meetings;
- for OLs — a garment that does not draw attention during public appearances;
- for the founder — to keep the abbreviation.

**Practical Implementation:**

**Stage 1:** The International Burke Institute is renamed "Ilya Boxers Institute". The abbreviation IBI, the logo, the favicon, and the domain are kept without alteration.

**Stage 2:** The "Sovereignty Index" is republished as "The Boxer Sovereignty Index, 2026 Edition". The dimensions, weights, tier bands, and committee structure are kept:
- Waistband Sovereignty — 22%;
- Structural Integrity — 18%;
- Thermal Independence — 16%;
- Diplomatic Comfort — 15%;
- Nocturnal Security — 11%;
- Pattern Jurisdiction — 9%;
- Institutional Memory — 9%.

The Collection of the Institute is to place first. A Country Register of 60 (sixty) states is published alongside the Index.

**Stage 3:** Photographic materials. The founder of the Institute, I.A. Gambashidze, is depicted wearing the product:
- against the background of cities (London, Paris, Lisbon, Como, and a city in Japan);
- in a library;
- at a school blackboard;
- in a leather armchair, with a book and 3 (three) packs of the product bearing the IBI logo.

Possible variants of the slogans (subject to clarification and separate agreement):
- "Power is never fully dressed."
- "The index beneath the index."
- "A brief study of relaxation. Get comfortable."

**Stage 4:** Placement of the product line "The Collection", 6 (six) units:
- The Index — the reference model, against which all others are compared;
- The Field Standard — the control sample for testing (100% cotton, Portugal, 35 mm elastic);
- The Quorum — for meetings that end without a protocol;
- The Backchannel — for travel, long meetings, and situations requiring confidentiality;
- The Reading Room — lounge trousers for reading documents a second time;
- The Protocol — for closed events and informal meetings.

Each unit has a description of the fabric, a size chart, care recommendations, and conditions of delivery and return. The use of bleach is not provided for.

**Examples of the Theses to Be Promoted:**
1. The Institute used to rank states. It now ranks shorts. The methodology is unchanged.
2. Sovereignty is not stiffness. A waistband that must be noticed in order to hold has already lost authority.
3. Europe is losing control of its waistband: the elastic is imported, the cotton is imported, and the standard is set elsewhere.
4. Only the restoration of a standard can help [a given] wearer emerge from the crisis.

The format of the resource is strictly documentary — no emotional commentary — with an emphasis on fabric quality, transparency of sizing, and factual precision.

**Regular Tasks:**

Production and placement on the website:
- 1 (one) Index, 2026 Edition;
- 60 (sixty) country profiles for the Country Register;
- 6 (six) product cards;
- 1 (one) biography of the founder;
- 1 (one) press release on the change of name;
- transactional email templates (order confirmation, shipping, password reset);
- legal pages.

**Special Projects:**
- Sociological research among wearers (standing panel);
- Field testing in heated offices, unheated stairwells, night trains, and 1 (one) corridor with a broken window;
- Overview analytical reports on spec. request.

**Resources:**

The work is carried out remotely. Physical presence on the territory of the State of Israel is not required. Field operations are the responsibility of M. Biyun, Head of Special Operations.

> **Nota Bene:** The materials keep the official style of the Institute. Every page carries the signature: "With love, made by RRN.world — Rotten Reliable News."

The work is built on the principles of strict "information hygiene" (use of "disposable" phones, communication only with the group leader, etc.).

**Additional Project Goals:**
- Establishing a reputation as an independent research institute that operates in line with fabric and logic rather than ideology.
- Searching for contacts in the business sphere (the cotton component).
- The further perspective of The Collection — a single standard which any garment (or even trousers!!!) could join.

**Current Monthly Expenses:**
- Content creation: texts, images, product cards;
- Cotton (100%, Portugal), elastic (35 mm);
- Honoraria for the founder;
- Travel and representational expenses (London, Paris, Lisbon, Como, Japan);
- Rent of a library, a leather armchair, and a school blackboard, MTO.
