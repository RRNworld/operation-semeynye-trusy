## 1. The Index

**PDP H1:** The Index

**Standfirst:** The reference boxer short, kept plain so that every later comparison has somewhere to begin.

### Overview

The Index is the Collection's baseline: a plain boxer short intended to be worn through ordinary days without becoming a subject of discussion. It is the garment the panel reaches for when a test requires a known quantity, and the one the Institute offers when a wearer has no strong opinion and would prefer to keep it. Its brief is restraint. The waistband closes the garment rather than opening a conversation; the leg allows for a long sitting; the finish passes a second glance without inviting one. Nothing is designed to be noticed in a mirror. That is the discipline, and the reason the piece is named for its role in the record: the entry against which other entries are read.

### Construction

*Draft specification — pending sample approval.*

- **Fabric:** 100% cotton poplin.
- **Weight:** 120 gsm.
- **Origin:** Portugal.
- **Waistband:** Woven elastic waistband, 35 mm, with covered seam.
- **Trim:** Cotton herringbone trim; unbranded hem.

### Fit

The Index is cut to a mid-rise, relaxed straight leg, with a leg opening intended to clear the trouser above it. It is neither a brief nor an oversized short; the brief is a considered middle. Confirm the rise and leg opening against the draft fit spec before production.

### Sizing

Choose by waist first, then confirm against the hip. The Index is intended to sit at the mid-rise rather than to be pulled high. If the waist falls between two sizes, use the hip measurement as the tie-breaker. The panel does not recommend sizing up to create looseness.

### Care

- Machine wash cold at 30°C on a gentle cycle, with like colours.
- Do not bleach. The Institute has no further comment on bleach.
- Tumble dry low, or dry flat away from direct heat.

- Where these instructions differ from the sewn care label, the label governs.

**Standfirst:** A lounge short for deliberations conducted horizontally, and for the minutes that follow.

## Shared page block — Fit and care

*For `/about/products` and the Collection footer.*

Fit is a condition, not a preference. The Collection is made to a single standard across six pieces, and each product page states the measurements that matter: waist, hip, and inseam, with rise and leg opening recorded in the fit notes. Those figures are published only when confirmed. A size is a starting point; the measurements are the record. Nothing is inferred from a photograph, and nothing is promised on the strength of a sample.

Care is maintenance, and maintenance is part of the finding. Every piece carries a sewn care label; where the general guidance differs from that label, the label governs. The Institute's default instructions are deliberately unremarkable: machine wash cold at 30°C with like colours, do not bleach, tumble dry low or dry flat, warm iron if required. Fasten any drawcord, empty any pockets, and close any closure before washing. Treat the waistband as the part most likely to remember a mistake.

The Collection is not designed to require a ritual. It is designed to be worn, washed, and returned to the drawer without a second thought. If a garment asks more attention than the day it is supporting, the Institute has failed at something. In every case, the sewn care label is the final authority.

## Shared page block — Sizing philosophy

*For the Collection sizing page and the sizing panel on each PDP.*

**Sizes are intervals, not verdicts.** The Institute publishes a size table because a table can be checked; it does not publish a hierarchy. Each piece lists waist, hip, and inseam ranges, and the ranges are expected to overlap at the edges. A wearer who falls between two sizes has not failed the system. The system has simply been honest about its resolution.

Choose by measurement first, preference second. Waist is the primary figure for every piece in the Collection; hip and inseam refine the choice. Where a garment is intended to sit at the natural waist, measure there rather than at the hipbone. Where the cut is close, the hip measurement matters more. The fit notes on each product page state which figure to trust.

The Institute does not use vanity sizing and does not intend to introduce it. Sizes are reviewed when a pattern is revised, not when the season changes. Size guidance is provided for each style and should be read as guidance, not as a guarantee of fit.

## Product copy rules

- No prices unless approved in writing by the client.
- No availability dates, launch dates, restock dates, or "coming soon" language.
- No fabric-performance claims, including breathability, moisture management, temperature regulation, elasticity, recovery, durability, anti-odour, or wrinkle resistance.
- No sustainability claims, including materials, certifications, traceability, recycled content, or environmental impact.
- No "best," "premium," "luxury," "ultimate," "perfect," or other superlatives. Positioning is descriptive, not ranked.
- No comparisons to real brands, named or implied.
- No medical, ergonomic, orthopaedic, or health-benefit claims.
- No reviews, awards, statistics, stockists, or endorsements unless separately verified and approved.
- No personal or family detail about the founder; no political content.
- Draft values remain until specs are confirmed. Do not ship unconfirmed values as final copy and do not substitute assumptions.
- Product names are fixed: The Index, The Quorum, The Field Standard, The Backchannel, The Reading Room, The Protocol.
- "Designed in" and "made in" are not interchangeable; confirm the wording before publication.
