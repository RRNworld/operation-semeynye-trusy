## National observation

Ireland enters the 2026 Edition with a composite of 83.5 and an Autonomous classification, placed fifteenth of sixty. The panel's assessment took place in rooms where damp is a household fact rather than a forecast: air that arrives wet, walls that hold it, and a drying rack that is never fully put away. Garments were judged less on how they looked when new than on how they behaved on the second day, when the waistband had been worn once and the hem had not quite dried.

Thermal Independence scored 88. The sample held a stable interior climate in a heated room and recovered slowly after a wet walk; the panel recorded that the garment was at its best once the outer layer came off. Structural Integrity scored 79, the profile's softest technical result. Seams remained sound, but hems lost their press after repeated indoor drying, and one waistband channel softened at the fold.

Laundry in the assessed sample was domestic, frequent, and cautious. Machines were small, cycles were short, and drying happened on racks near radiators or in a hot press. Repair was ordinary: hems were re-stitched, elastic was replaced, and a garment was kept for the garden before it was retired. Institutional Memory scored 76, reflecting a garment that softened honestly but did not always keep its line. Retail offered plain weaves and small checks in equal measure. Composite: 83.5, fifteenth of sixty.

## Field note

Field note — Galway, November. Assessment conducted in a terraced house with a hot press, a drying rack in the back room, and rain on the windows for most of the first day. Ambient conditions: 18°C indoors, 71 per cent relative humidity at 08:00. The sample was worn through a seated morning, a wet walk of twenty minutes, and an evening by a radiator. It was washed at 30°C and dried indoors over two days. The waistband required one adjustment after the walk; the hem remained slightly damp at 22:00 and had dried by morning. The assessor recorded that the garment was more comfortable on the second day, and left it at that.
